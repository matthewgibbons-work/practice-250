Bolt & Bucket Fan Club

Saturdays at 8 am

Two robots, one junkyard, and a whole lot of beeping. Bolt is fast and always sure of himself. Bucket is slow, careful, and usually right. Together they fix whatever the week has broken.

Meet the robots

Bolt

Built from a toaster and a pogo stick. Bolt rushes into every problem and bounces out the other side.

Bucket

A rusty old mop bucket with a very big brain. Bucket thinks before he rolls.
