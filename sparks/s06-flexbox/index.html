Nov 13: Space Night

The Comet That Came to Supper starts at sundown, about 5:15. Beach Party on Planet Nine follows at 7:00. Bring a blanket: the space movies always run cold.

Nov 20: Monster Night

Them Ants! at 5:10, then The Lonely Swamp at 6:55. Bring a flashlight for the walk to the snack bar, if you're brave.

Nov 27: Cowboy Night

Showdown at Dry Creek at 5:05, then The Last Stagecoach at 6:45. Hats are welcome; please take them off once the picture starts.

Dec 4: Holiday Night

Snowed In for Christmas at 5:00, then Santa's Night Off at 6:40. Cocoa is free with any snack bar order, so bring a thermos.
