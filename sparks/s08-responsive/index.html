Winter Club Fair

Forty clubs, one hallway. Here's when the most-asked-about ones meet, so you can check your schedule before you sign up.

New at the fair this year

  • Board Game Night

    Bring a game or borrow one of theirs. No experience needed, and nobody minds if you show up late.

  • Spanish Conversation

    A lunch hour of talking, not grammar drills. Every level sits together, so beginners hear fluent speakers.

  • Film Society

    One classic film every Saturday, then fifteen minutes of talking about it. Dues cover the popcorn.

How to sign up

  1. Find the club's table. They're grouped by kind: sports, arts, languages, then games.
  2. Scan the QR code on the table, or write your name and email on the sheet.
  3. Go to the first meeting. Most clubs collect dues there, not at the fair.

Can't decide?

Free clubs let you try a meeting or two before you join. Ask at the table.

Missed the fair?

The Student Activities desk on the Wilkinson Center's third floor has every club's contact.
