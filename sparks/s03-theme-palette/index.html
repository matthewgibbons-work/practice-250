Saturday Morning Cereal

About this theme

This palette is a 1990s cereal box at 7 am on a Saturday: loud, sugary, and a little ridiculous. The red is the box that shouts from the shelf, and the yellow is the prize inside. The cream is a bowl of milk and the deep grape is the cartoon outline that holds it all together. It's made for phones between classes, so every color can be loud while the text stays easy to read.
