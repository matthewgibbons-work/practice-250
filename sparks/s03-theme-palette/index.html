Saturday-Morning Cereal Box

A style tile for a bright, retro cartoon theme.

About this theme

This palette borrows from the cereal boxes on the table while the cartoons played: a warm cream like the inside of the box, a deep navy for the words, a tomato red for anything you can click, and a sunny yellow for the prize inside. It should feel loud and happy at a glance. The navy keeps it readable, even on a phone in sunlight. I'll reuse it as the start of my P1 show page.

The palette

    Role: highlight (behind text)
