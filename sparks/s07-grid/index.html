Hikes near campus

Four trails within a half-hour of BYU, from a quick evening climb to a Saturday waterfall.

Pick a hike

  • Y Mountain Trail

    Switchbacks straight up to the block Y above campus. Short, steep, and the best view of the valley for the effort.

    About 2.4 miles round trip · Hard · 1–2 hours

    Directions to the Y trailhead

  • Rock Canyon

    A wide, rocky canyon trail at the north end of Provo. Walk in as far as you like and turn around when you're done.

    1–6 miles, your choice · Easy to moderate · 1–3 hours

    Directions to Rock Canyon

  • Stewart Falls

    A shaded trail through aspens to a two-tier waterfall below Mount Timpanogos. Worth the drive up Provo Canyon.

    About 3.4 miles round trip · Moderate · 2–3 hours

    Directions to Stewart Falls

  • Battle Creek Falls

    A short climb in Pleasant Grove to a falls you can stand right beside. Good for a first hike with friends.

    About 1.8 miles round trip · Moderate · 1 hour

    Directions to Battle Creek Falls

Before you go

Parking

Every trailhead lot is small. On Saturdays, arrive before 9 a.m. or carpool.

Trail conditions

November mornings can be icy on the shady switchbacks. Microspikes help on the Y and Stewart Falls.

Bring

  • Water (more than you think)
  • A layer for the wind at the top
  • A headlamp if you start after 3 p.m.
