Starlight Snack Bar

Tonight's menu

  • Popcorn, small or bucket: 50¢ or $1
  • Foot-long hot dog: 75¢
  • Root beer float: 60¢
  • Chocolate malt: 65¢

How to order

Walk up to the window between features, or flash your parking lights twice and a carhop will come to you. The grill closes ten minutes before the second show starts, so get your hot dogs early.

Prices from opening night, 1957.
